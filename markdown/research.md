### Chaochen Wang Lab — Zhejiang University

**High-Fat Diet Impact on Mammary Gland Development**

Role: Project leader

- Built ND/HFD mouse models across puberty, generated whole-mount staining and snRNA-seq data
- Profiled dysregulated gene co-expression modules with WGCNA/NMF
- Dissected Areg→Ghr regulation via ADAM17 blockade and qPCR/Western
- Identified Map2+Adam12+ fibroblasts as HFD-activated collagen producers
- Mapped adipocyte–microenvironment crosstalk (pseudotime, CellChat)
